Class Guidelines

Before You Attend

🧘 Arrive Early

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your class begins.

👕 Comfortable Clothing

Wear light and comfortable clothing suitable for yoga.

🧴 Bring Your Mat

Carry your own yoga mat and water bottle whenever possible.

📵 Silent Mode

Please keep mobile phones on silent during sessions.

❤️ Health Information

Inform your instructor if you have any medical conditions.

😊 Positive Mindset

Come with an open mind and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere.
