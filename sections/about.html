Our Story

Rooted in Tradition, Growing with You

Arivukadal Sky Yoga was founded with a clear vision: to help every person — from a curious seven-year-old to a senior seeking gentle movement — discover a healthier, more peaceful life through authentic yoga and meditation.

Our programs blend ancient wisdom with practical, age-appropriate techniques that improve physical health, mental clarity and emotional balance. Whether you are a student managing exam stress, a working professional chasing calm, or an elder seeking strength and stillness, you will find your place here.

  • Traditional Yoga & Meditation Practices
  • Classes for Children, Adults & Senior Practitioners
  • Holistic Wellness & Healthy Lifestyle Guidance
  • Experienced & Caring Instructors
